NP Membership Benefits

NP members receive community calendar listings, nonprofit networking invitations, and access to Chamber training sessions.

  • Free membership for qualifying nonprofits
  • Invitation to community partnership events
  • Listing in the chamber directory

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze membership helps small businesses build visibility and learn from local business leaders.

  • Everything in NP membership
  • Discounted admission to select Chamber events
  • Access to business training workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver membership expands marketing opportunities and gives members a stronger presence in chamber promotions.

  • Everything in Bronze membership
  • Featured placement in rotating chamber promotions
  • Additional discounts on sponsorships and events

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold membership delivers top-tier exposure and premium engagement opportunities throughout the year.

  • Everything in Silver membership
  • Spotlight placement on the home page
  • VIP invitations and premier event discounts
